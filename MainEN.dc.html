01 — CONCEPT

Surrounded by beautiful things,
simply being here.

Villa Asakura is a place to stay woven together by two generations of beauty professionals: a hair designer whose career spanned New York, Milan and Paris, and a curator who studied at Central Saint Martins in London.

Marble and furniture commissioned from Italian workshops. Pieces gathered from across the seas. Contemporary art from Japan and abroad. And outside the windows, 270 degrees of the Seto Inland Sea — not even a road in sight.

Time spent here becomes a work of art in itself.

04 — ART

Every stay,
a new encounter with art.

Throughout the villa are contemporary art and antiques chosen by the curator from Japan and abroad. The tatami room, whose Aji stone wall continues outside, was designed around the theme of “the Japanese room Westerners imagine.”

If a piece stays with you, you can take it home.
