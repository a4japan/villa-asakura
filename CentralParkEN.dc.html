01 — THE ROOM

An atrium, a skylight,
a hideaway in the middle of town.

A maisonette across the fourth and fifth floors, with light falling five metres through a skylight. It looks onto the green of Central Park, and though the nightlife is a step away, the nights are remarkably quiet.

Interiors the owners chose abroad, and contemporary art from Japan and elsewhere. For families, friends, and guests from overseas.

FLOOR GUIDE

Tap a room

The fourth floor holds the living room and a bedroom beneath the atrium. Up the spiral stair, the fifth has the adjustable-bed room and the bathroom. Hover over (or tap) a room on the plan for photos and details.

BICYCLE RENTAL

Shared bikes

1 min walk

A shared-bike port is about a one-minute walk away. To explore the city by bike, simply download an app such as LUUP — overseas guests can use it as is (no driver's licence needed; credit card registration required).

PARKING

Parking

12-hour tickets

We can prepare 12-hour tickets for the nearby "Marui Kame-san" car park (丸い亀さん。, Marugamemachi Municipal Parking No. 4), one for each night of your stay. Please let us know by message at least 3 days before arrival.

The tickets are bought in advance, so we cannot arrange them after that.
