03 — SPRING ・ KONPIRA KABUKI

In an Edo playhouse,
kabuki, in spring alone.

Kanamaruza in Kotohira, built in 1835, is the oldest surviving playhouse in Japan and a nationally designated Important Cultural Property. Each spring, leading kabuki actors gather here for the Shikoku Konpira Grand Kabuki.

The stage machinery is still worked by hand. The lighting is daylight, let in by opening and closing the shutters. From a box seat, the actors on the hanamichi are almost within reach — the same experience an Edo audience had, and one no large theatre can give.

Before the run, the actors parade through town by rickshaw. In spring, the whole of Kotohira becomes the playhouse.
