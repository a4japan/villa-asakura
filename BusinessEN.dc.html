FOR HR ・ for admin and HR

Questions we're often asked

How are expenses split?

Typically the company covers travel and accommodation on working days, while extra nights and sightseeing are personal. We issue itemised, separate receipts.

Are injuries during the leave portion covered by workers' compensation?

Injuries while sightseeing outside working hours may not be covered. We recommend travel insurance where appropriate.

What do we need for internal approval?

We send a quote, facility overview and itinerary as PDFs.
