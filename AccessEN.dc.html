BUS ・ PROMENADE × CONNECT

From the station to the town centre,
every 30 minutes.

The Machinaka Direct Bus links JR Takamatsu Station and Sunport Takamatsu with the central shopping arcades. For CENTRAL PARK and ATELIER, get off at Marugamemachi Green and walk a short way through the arcade. Staying at RESORT, it is an easy way into town from the station.

02 — FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What are the check-in and check-out times?

CENTRAL PARK and ATELIER: check in 15:00, check out 11:00. For RESORT, just let us know your plans when you book — we can be flexible.

Is there parking?

RESORT has private parking on the ground floor of the building. For CENTRAL PARK and ATELIER, we can prepare 12-hour tickets for the nearby "Marui Kame-san" car park (Marugamemachi Municipal Parking No. 4). Please let us know by message at least 3 days before arrival.

Can we stay with children?

Very welcome. Please keep an eye on small children near the open atrium.

What do overseas guests need to do?

Under Japan's Hotel Business Act, we ask for details of every guest and a copy of each passport in advance.

What is the cancellation policy?

For bookings made on Airbnb, the cancellation policy shown on the Airbnb booking page applies.

What languages do you speak?

We can help you in Japanese, English and Chinese.

03 — POLICIES

Terms and licences

Booking & cancellation

Bookings are taken through Airbnb. The terms of stay and cancellation policy shown on the Airbnb booking page apply.

House rules

No smoking anywhere in the building, and no pets. Only registered guests may enter, and parties or events are not permitted. If furnishings or artworks are damaged or lost, we may charge the actual cost.

Privacy policy

Personal information you send us with an enquiry (your name, email address, stay requests and so on) is used only to reply to you and arrange your stay. Except where required by law, it is never shared with third parties without your consent. Questions about personal information: info@a4tokyo.com
