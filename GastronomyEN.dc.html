GASTRONOMY

The Seto Inland Sea, on a table set for you alone.

01 — PHILOSOPHY

There is no
set menu.

A chef who knows Kagawa's sea and soil takes the kitchen. Fish from the Inland Sea, olive-fed beef, Sanuki vegetables and fruit. A menu for one night, built from whatever is best that week.

Vegan, gluten-free and allergy requirements accommodated with advance notice.

02 — THE CHEFS

Japanese, or Italian.
Two chefs.

A Japanese chef who knows the season on this sea, and an Italian chef in love with Kagawa's ingredients. Choose whichever suits the evening.

03 — COURSE

A sample menu

Kagawa is rich in seafood, fruit and vegetables alike. The menu shifts with the season and the market — whatever is best that week, served at its best.

SETOUCHI KAISEKI

Setouchi kaiseki

A seasonal kaiseki built on local fish and Sanuki produce.

  • SakizukeSeasonal Sanuki vegetables with sesame tofu
  • WanmonoSea bream from the Inland Sea in clear broth
  • MukozukeSashimi of that morning's catch (sea bream, yellowtail, seasonal fish)
  • YakimonoSpanish mackerel grilled in Saikyo miso, or the seasonal grilled fish
  • ShiizakanaSeared Kagawa olive-fed beef with Shodoshima soy sauce
  • Rice courseKagawa rice cooked in a clay pot, pickles, red miso soup
  • FruitSeasonal Kagawa fruit

SETOUCHI ITALIANO

Setouchi Italian

What the sea and islands give, through Italian technique.

  • AntipastoCarpaccio of local fish with Shodoshima olive oil
  • PrimoHand-made pasta of Sanuki wheat with Inland Sea shellfish
  • PescePan-seared sea bream with seasonal vegetables
  • CarneGrilled Kagawa olive-fed beef
  • DolceDolce of seasonal Kagawa fruit
  • CaffèEspresso to finish

A sample only. Allergies, dislikes, vegan and gluten-free requirements accommodated with advance notice.

04 — RYOTEI BENTO

A ryotei bento,
for mornings and for the road.

Working with a Takamatsu ryotei, we can arrange catered bento — for mornings when a chef is more than you need, or lunch on a day out.

MORNING SHIDASHI

Morning bento from the ryotei

Delivered to your room from a Takamatsu ryotei. A Japanese breakfast scented with dashi, facing the sea.

  • Advance booking required
  • Delivery time arranged to suit you

LUNCH BOX

A lunch box to take with you

Take a ryotei bento island-hopping or sightseeing. On the ferry, in a park, with the Inland Sea in view.

  • Prepared on advance request

05 — ON REQUEST

Whatever you'd like,
from what Kagawa gives.

Alongside a meal, or at any point in your stay. Kagawa sake, fruit and sweets, arranged to your wishes.

SAKE & WINE

Kagawa sake

Kagawa sake, Setouchi wine and craft beer. We can suggest a bottle to match the meal.

FRUIT

Seasonal fruit

Sanukihime strawberries, kiwi, Shine Muscat. Kagawa is full of fruit — we bring the best of the season to your room.

SWEETS

Sweets and cakes

Birthday and anniversary cakes, local wagashi and pastries, arranged on request.
